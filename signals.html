Every EXCELLENT-band signal the scanner has logged, resolved automatically against the candle path that actually followed it. Entry/SL are the model's real numbers; TP1-3 are a third of the target, two-thirds, and the full target (~4% move), shown as levels rather than just an R-multiple. Two exit plans are scored from the same signals: A takes a third off at each TP and moves the stop to breakeven after TP1; B holds full size to TP3. Nothing here is hand-marked — entry, stop and target are fixed at signal time and never edited.
